2. Physical Access Security, RFID Badges & Guest Protocols

2.1 Corporate RFID Security Badges

Physical campus security is foundational to protecting proprietary intellectual property, customer data assets, and employee safety. Every employee is issued a personalized, photo-identified corporate RFID security badge during onboarding:

  • Mandatory Display: Badges must be visibly worn and displayed on lanyards or clip-ons at all times while on corporate property.
  • Anti-Tailgating Protocol: Tailgating (entering behind another individual without tapping an authorized badge) is strictly prohibited. Every employee must tap their individual badge at card readers when entering turnstiles, exterior perimeter doors, and restricted server rooms.
  • Lost Badges: Lost or stolen badges must be reported immediately (within two hours) to Security via security@globaltech.internal so the badge token can be electronically deactivated.

2.2 Visitor Management & Guest Escorts

All external guests (including vendors, candidates, clients, and personal family visitors) must be registered in the Envoy visitor management system at building reception upon arrival. Guests must present valid government photo identification, execute a standard Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), and wear a visible temporary guest badge. Visitors must be escorted by their GlobalTech host at all times within non-public office zones.

3. Flexible Hot-Desking & Workspace Reservation Systems

3.1 The GlobalTech Workspaces Mobile App

To support our hybrid and flexible working models, GlobalTech utilizes an agile hot-desking framework across all campuses, managed through the GlobalTech Workspaces mobile app and desktop portal:

  • Desk Booking: Employees planning to work from an office hub may reserve an ergonomic sit-stand desk up to fourteen (14) days in advance. Reservations should be made at least 24 hours prior to arrival.
  • Check-in Requirement: Upon arrival, the employee must scan the QR code located on the desk surface using their mobile app within thirty (30) minutes of their scheduled booking start time. Unclaimed desk reservations are automatically released into the general pool.
  • Dedicated Desks: Dedicated assigned desks are reserved exclusively for employees classified as "On-Site Essential" or individuals requiring specific ergonomic accommodations certified by People Operations.

3.2 Conference Room Booking & 15-Minute Auto-Release Policy

Meeting rooms are scheduled via Google Calendar. To maximize shared room availability, GlobalTech enforces an automated **15-minute auto-release policy**: if an in-room presence sensor or Zoom Room session is not initiated within fifteen (15) minutes of the scheduled meeting start time, the calendar reservation is automatically cancelled and the room becomes available for impromptu walk-up use.

4. Commuter Benefits, Transportation & Wellness Amenities

4.1 Pre-Tax Commuter Transit & Parking FSA ($315 USD / Month)

In accordance with IRS Section 132(f), GlobalTech enables all regular employees to elect pre-tax payroll deductions up to the statutory limit of $315.00 USD per month for eligible public mass transit (subways, commuter rail, vanpools, ferries) and an additional $315.00 USD per month for qualified commercial parking near an office facility.

4.2 Bicycle Storage, Showers & Sustainable Transit

Every GlobalTech facility provides complimentary, secure, card-access indoor bicycle storage rooms equipped with repair stands and electric bike charging stations. Locker rooms with private showers, complimentary towel service, and eco-friendly toiletries are provided to support active cycling, running, and walking commuters.

5. Severe Weather, Emergency Office Closures & Communications

5.1 Emergency Mass Notification System (Everbridge)

In the event of severe weather emergencies (blizzards, hurricanes, earthquakes), municipal infrastructure failures, civil disruptions, or facility emergencies, GlobalTech activates the **Everbridge Emergency Mass Notification System** to dispatch simultaneous SMS text alerts, automated voice calls, and push notifications to all affected regional staff.

5.2 Remote Transition During Office Closures

When a physical office is closed due to emergency circumstances, all scheduled hybrid and office-centric employees are expected to transition smoothly to remote work from home, provided it is safe to do so. In the event of widespread regional power outages or natural disasters preventing home remote work, employees will receive full regular pay for the duration of the emergency closure without deducting from their accrued PTO balances.
