# GlobalTech Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-EXP-2024  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** April 5, 2024  
**Category:** Finance & Operations  
**Audience:** All GlobalTech Employees Submitting Business Expenses  

---

## 1. Core Principles
All business expenses incurred on behalf of GlobalTech must be reasonable, necessary, appropriately documented, and directly aligned with bona fide corporate objectives.

## 2. Travel Booking & Airfare Guidelines
1. **Corporate Portal:** All airline tickets, lodging, and rental cars must be booked through the Navan/TripActions corporate booking platform.
2. **Airfare Class:**
   - **Domestic & Short-Haul International (under 6 hours flight time):** Standard Coach/Economy class is mandatory.
   - **Long-Haul International (6+ continuous flight hours):** Premium Economy is permitted. Business class is permitted only for transatlantic or transpacific travel with prior written CFO approval.
3. **Advance Booking:** Flights must be booked at least **14 calendar days in advance** of departure whenever feasible to secure standard commercial fares.

## 3. Daily Meal Allowances (Per Diem Caps)
GlobalTech reimburses meals on actual expenses incurred up to the following daily per diem maximums:
- **Breakfast:** Up to **$25.00 USD**
- **Lunch:** Up to **$35.00 USD**
- **Dinner:** Up to **$65.00 USD**
- **Daily Combined Meal Ceiling:** **$125.00 USD** (including tax and tip).
- **Gratuity:** Tips exceeding 20% will not be reimbursed.
- **Alcohol:** Alcoholic beverages for individual dining are not reimbursable. Alcohol is only eligible when entertaining clients or hosting team events, requiring senior director pre-approval.

## 4. Lodging & Ground Transportation
1. **Hotel Rates:** Standard maximum reimbursement rate is **$250.00 USD per night** (before local taxes and fees) for standard tier cities, and up to **$350.00 USD per night** for designated high-cost markets (e.g., San Francisco, New York City, London, Tokyo).
2. **Ground Travel:** Employees should prioritize public transit or standard rideshare (UberX, Lyft standard). Luxury vehicle options (Uber Black, XL unless sharing with 3+ colleagues) are non-reimbursable. Personal mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate (67 cents/mile for 2024).

## 5. Expense Submission Deadlines & Approval Thresholds
1. **Submission Window:** All expense reports must be submitted via Concur within **30 calendar days** of the transaction date. Reports submitted beyond 60 days will be rejected.
2. **Receipt Requirement:** Itemized receipts showing date, merchant, item breakdown, and amount are mandatory for all single transactions equal to or exceeding **$25.00 USD**. Credit card charge slips without itemization are insufficient.
3. **Approval Authority Tiers:**
   - **Tier 1 (Up to $1,000 USD):** Direct Manager approval.
   - **Tier 2 ($1,001 to $5,000 USD):** Department Vice President approval.
   - **Tier 3 (Above $5,000 USD):** Chief Financial Officer (CFO) approval.

## 6. Strictly Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following expenditures will NOT be reimbursed under any circumstances:
- Travel, accommodation, or meal expenses incurred during personal PTO or international remote workations.
- Personal entertainment (in-flight Wi-Fi for personal streaming, mini-bar snacks, hotel movies, spa services).
- Fines, parking tickets, speeding citations, or towing fees.
- Airline seat upgrades or seat selection fees (unless mandated for medical accommodation).
- Lost baggage, personal property insurance, or passport/visa renewal fees for personal tourism.
