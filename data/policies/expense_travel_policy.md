# GlobalTech Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-EXP-2024  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** April 5, 2024  
**Category:** Finance & Operations  
**Audience:** All GlobalTech Employees Submitting Business Expenses  
**Policy Owner:** Corporate Finance, Accounting & Travel Operations  

---

## 1. Core Principles and Fiduciary Responsibilities
All business expenses incurred on behalf of GlobalTech must be reasonable, necessary, appropriately documented, and directly aligned with bona fide corporate objectives. GlobalTech operates with high fiscal responsibility and fiduciary integrity. Team members are expected to exercise sound judgment when spending company resources, seeking economical arrangements that meet business goals while avoiding unnecessary, extravagant, or personal expenditures.

This comprehensive policy defines the binding procedures, allowable expenditure limits, receipt documentation standards, approval authorization hierarchies, and strict disallowances governing all business travel, corporate entertainment, professional development, and employee expense reimbursements.

---

## 2. Corporate Travel Booking & Airfare Guidelines

### 2.1 Mandatory Booking Through Corporate Travel Management Platform
- All corporate airline reservations, rail travel, commercial lodging, and rental vehicles must be booked directly through the **Navan (formerly TripActions)** enterprise portal.
- Bookings made through consumer websites (Expedia, Booking.com, Airbnb, personal airline portals) without prior written authorization from the Travel Operations team will not be eligible for corporate expense reimbursement.
- Direct booking is permitted exclusively in the event of severe emergency flight disruptions when the Navan concierge desk is unreachable.

### 2.2 Commercial Airfare Service Classes & Cabin Standards
1. **Domestic & Short-Haul International Flights (Under 6 Continuous Flight Hours):**
   - **Standard Coach / Economy Class** is mandatory for all domestic flights and short-haul international flights where total gate-to-gate flight time is under six (6) hours.
   - Basic Economy tickets (which disallow carry-on baggage and advance seat assignment) should be avoided to prevent operational delays.
2. **Long-Haul Transoceanic & Continental Flights (6+ Continuous Flight Hours):**
   - **Premium Economy Class** (e.g., Delta Comfort+, United Premium Plus, British Airways World Traveller Plus) is authorized for non-stop flights exceeding six (6) continuous hours.
   - **Business Class Travel:** Business class travel is strictly limited to transatlantic or transpacific long-haul flights exceeding ten (10) continuous flight hours AND requires prior written sign-off from both the Department Executive Vice President and the **Chief Financial Officer (CFO)**. First-class travel is strictly prohibited under all circumstances.
3. **Advance Booking Requirement (Minimum 14 Days):**
   - Flights must be ticketed at least **fourteen (14) calendar days in advance** of departure whenever business scheduling permits to secure standard corporate fare brackets.
   - Bookings made inside the 14-day window require a written business justification in Navan explaining client scheduling urgency.

## 3. Daily Meal Allowances (Per Diem Caps) and Dining Standards

GlobalTech reimburses meals on an actual expense reimbursement basis (not an automatic lump-sum stipend) up to established daily per diem maximum thresholds.

### 3.1 Daily Per Diem Maximum Caps ($125 USD Combined Total)
| Meal Category | Maximum Allowable Threshold | Description & Conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Breakfast** | **$25.00 USD** | Applicable when travel begins prior to 7:00 AM or includes an overnight stay. |
| **Lunch** | **$35.00 USD** | Applicable during full-day travel or off-site business meetings. |
| **Dinner** | **$65.00 USD** | Applicable when travel extends past 7:30 PM local time. |
| **Combined Daily Ceiling** | **$125.00 USD** | Maximum aggregate total allowable per 24-hour travel day (including tax and gratuity). |

### 3.2 Gratuity Guidelines
- Gratuity (tips) for restaurant waitstaff, food delivery, and taxi services is reimbursable up to a maximum of **twenty percent (20%)** of the subtotal bill.
- Tips exceeding 20% will be deducted from the reimbursable total and must be covered personally by the employee.

### 3.3 Alcohol Reimbursement Policies
- **Individual Dining:** Alcoholic beverages consumed during solo employee dining are **strictly non-reimbursable**. Personal beer, wine, or cocktails purchased while dining alone must be requested on a separate check and paid out-of-pocket.
- **Client Entertainment & Team Dinners:** Moderate alcohol consumption (beer and wine) is reimbursable exclusively during formal client entertainment dinners or official manager-hosted team recognition dinners, provided the dinner has a clear commercial agenda and has been pre-approved by a Senior Director or VP. Excessive alcohol consumption is a direct violation of the Code of Business Conduct.

## 4. Commercial Lodging & Ground Transportation Guidelines

### 4.1 Hotel Rate Tiers & Booking Thresholds
GlobalTech reimburses standard commercial hotel accommodations based on market geography:
- **Standard Tier Markets:** Up to **$250.00 USD per night** (excluding mandatory local occupancy taxes and municipal tourism fees) for standard metropolitan areas (e.g., Austin, Denver, Atlanta, Raleigh, Chicago).
- **Designated High-Cost Tier Markets:** Up to **$350.00 USD per night** (excluding taxes) for designated high-cost financial centers: San Francisco, New York City, London, Tokyo, Zurich, Singapore, and Paris.
- **Room Types:** Standard single-occupancy rooms only. Suites, executive penthouses, and club-level upgrades are non-reimbursable.
- **Alternative Lodging (Airbnb / VRBO):** Authorized through Navan only when commercial hotel rates exceed policy caps by more than 30% or for team offsites where whole-house rentals deliver verified corporate cost savings.

### 4.2 Ground Transportation & Rideshare Services
- **Public Transit:** Employees are strongly encouraged to utilize clean, rapid public transit (subways, commuter trains, airport express links) whenever practical.
- **Rideshare Services:** Standard rideshare services (UberX, Lyft Standard) are authorized for business transit to/from airports, hotels, and client sites. Premium luxury vehicle tiers (Uber Black, Uber Select, Lyft Lux) are **strictly non-reimbursable**. XL vehicles are authorized only when transporting three (3) or more GlobalTech colleagues or bulky conference booth equipment.
- **Personal Automobile Mileage:** When employees utilize personal motor vehicles for authorized business travel, GlobalTech reimburses mileage at the prevailing United States Internal Revenue Service (IRS) standard mileage rate (**$0.67 USD per mile for 2024**). Mileage reimbursement covers all fuel, oil, wear-and-tear, maintenance, and insurance costs. Routine daily commuting between home and assigned office is non-reimbursable.
- **Rental Cars:** Intermediate or compact sedan rentals through corporate contracted vendors (Enterprise, National, Hertz) are permitted only when public transit or rideshare is demonstrably cost-ineffective. GlobalTech maintains comprehensive corporate collision damage waiver (CDW) coverage; employees should decline optional rental counter insurance when renting in the United States.

## 5. Expense Submission Deadlines, Receipt Thresholds, and Approvals

### 5.1 Submission Window (30 Calendar Days)
- All business expense reports must be submitted through the SAP Concur expense portal within **thirty (30) calendar days** following the date the expense was incurred (or within 30 days of returning from travel).
- Reports submitted between 31 and 60 days require Department VP justification.
- **Forfeiture:** Any expense submitted more than **sixty (60) calendar days** after the transaction date will be automatically rejected and is ineligible for corporate reimbursement under IRS accountable plan rules.

### 5.2 Mandatory Receipt Threshold ($25.00 USD)
- In strict adherence to IRS accountable plan regulations and internal audit standards, **itemized merchant receipts are mandatory for any individual expenditure equal to or exceeding $25.00 USD**.
- An itemized receipt must show:
  1. The name and physical address of the vendor/merchant.
  2. The exact transaction date and time.
  3. An itemized breakdown of goods/services purchased (e.g., specific food items, not simply "Food &amp; Beverage").
  4. The total amount paid, currency, and payment card confirmation.
- Generic credit card summary slips (showing only the grand total without line-item detail) or handwritten notes do not satisfy compliance requirements.
- Expenditures under $25.00 USD (e.g., subway fares, road tolls, small coffee purchases) do not require a physical receipt unless mandated by local country tax laws.

### 5.3 Approval Authority Tiers & Signature Matrix
| Approval Tier | Expense Report Value | Mandatory Approving Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | Up to **$1,000.00 USD** | Direct Hiring Manager / Engineering Lead |
| **Tier 2** | **$1,001.00 to $5,000.00 USD** | Department Vice President (VP) |
| **Tier 3** | Above **$5,000.00 USD** | Chief Financial Officer (CFO) |

No employee may approve their own expense report, nor may an employee approve the expense report of an immediate supervisor or executive superior.

## 6. Client Entertainment, Team Events, and Business Meals
- **Business Justification:** When expensing client dining or vendor entertainment, the Concur report must explicitly list:
  1. The full names, titles, and company affiliations of all attendees.
  2. The specific commercial business topic discussed or transaction pursued.
- **Per-Person Ceilings:** Business meals entertaining prospective clients or active partners are capped at **$125.00 USD per attendee** including tax and gratuity. Meals exceeding $125/person require advance Department VP sign-off.
- **Team Celebrations:** Quarterly team offsites and project completion dinners are capped at **$75.00 USD per team member** and require prior budget allocation from the Department Head.

## 7. Strictly Non-Reimbursable Expenditures

The following expenditures are deemed strictly personal and will **NOT be reimbursed under any circumstances**:
1. **Personal PTO & Remote Workations:** Any travel, airfare, rail, lodging, or meal expenses incurred during personal PTO or temporary international remote workations (**POL-REMOTE-2024**, Section 5).
2. **Traffic & Parking Fines:** Traffic citations, speeding tickets, red-light camera fines, vehicle towing fees, or parking violations.
3. **Personal Entertainment:** In-flight streaming media (Netflix, Hulu), hotel in-room movies, gaming, mini-bar snacks, health club fees, golf green fees, or spa treatments.
4. **Airline Comfort Upgrades:** Preferred seat selection fees, extra legroom charges, early boarding upgrades, or airline lounge day passes (unless pre-approved for senior executives).
5. **Personal Care:** Toiletries, clothing, shoe shines, haircuts, dry cleaning (unless business travel exceeds 5 consecutive business days).
6. **Lost Property:** Loss or theft of personal electronic devices, cash, jewelry, or luggage not covered by airline compensation.
7. **Credit Card Penalties:** Late payment fees, annual credit card membership fees, or interest charges incurred on personal credit cards.
8. **Charitable Contributions & Political Donations:** Any personal donations made in an individual capacity.
