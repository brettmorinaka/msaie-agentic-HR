1. Enrollment Windows and Eligibility Framework

GlobalTech provides industry-leading, comprehensive healthcare, dental, vision, life, disability, retirement, and wellness benefits designed to support team members and their families across every phase of life. Benefits eligibility commences on the employee's first official day of active employment (**Day 1 coverage**).

1.1 New Hire Enrollment Window (30 Calendar Days)

Newly hired employees must finalize and submit their benefit elections through the Workday Benefits Portal within thirty (30) calendar days from their official start date. If an employee fails to submit elections within this mandatory 30-day window, they will be automatically defaulted into the Employee-Only Standard PPO Medical Plan, Core Dental, and Basic Life Insurance, and will waive flexible spending accounts and dependent coverage for the remainder of the plan year.

1.2 Annual Open Enrollment Period (November 1 – November 20)

The annual Open Enrollment period takes place each autumn from November 1 through November 20. During this window, employees may freely modify plan tiers, add or remove eligible dependents, switch between PPO and HDHP medical options, and establish new annual contribution limits for Flexible Spending Accounts (FSAs) and Health Savings Accounts (HSAs). All elections finalized during Open Enrollment become effective on January 1 of the following calendar year.

1.3 Qualifying Life Events (QLE)

Outside of Open Enrollment, employees may only modify their existing benefit elections within thirty (30) calendar days of experiencing a recognized Qualifying Life Event (QLE) under Section 125 of the Internal Revenue Code. Qualifying events include:

  • Legal marriage, registration of a domestic partnership, legal separation, or divorce.
  • Birth of a child, legal adoption, or foster child placement.
  • Death of an enrolled spouse, domestic partner, or dependent child.
  • Involuntary loss of existing healthcare coverage by an employee or spouse through job termination or reduction in hours.
  • Significant change in employment status affecting benefits eligibility (e.g., transition from part-time to full-time).

Employees must provide official supporting documentation (such as a certified marriage certificate, birth certificate, or letter of creditable coverage loss) via the HR portal within the 30-day window.

2. Comprehensive Medical Insurance Plans

GlobalTech partners with BlueCross BlueShield (BCBS) to offer three robust medical coverage choices tailored to diverse healthcare requirements and financial preferences:

2.1 The Premier PPO Plan

The Premier PPO Plan is a premium, low-deductible preferred provider organization plan offering comprehensive nationwide in-network access without requiring primary care physician (PCP) referrals for specialist consultations.

  • Annual Deductible: $500.00 USD for Individual coverage; $1,000.00 USD for Family coverage.
  • In-Network Preventative Care: 100% covered with $0 copayment (annual well-checks, mammograms, colonoscopies, immunizations, and routine pediatric screenings).
  • In-Network Primary & Specialist Care: Flat $25.00 copayment for primary care; $40.00 copayment for specialists; 90% coinsurance after deductible for advanced outpatient imaging (MRI, CT scans) and surgical procedures.
  • Out-of-Pocket Maximum: Capped at $2,500.00 USD Individual and $5,000.00 USD Family per calendar year. Once met, all covered in-network services are paid at 100% by the plan.
  • Prescription Drug Benefits: Tier 1 Generic: $10 copay; Tier 2 Preferred Brand: $30 copay; Tier 3 Non-Preferred Brand: $60 copay; Specialty Medications: 20% coinsurance up to $150 maximum per 30-day supply.

2.2 The High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA

The HDHP combines catastrophic medical security with a triple-tax-advantaged Health Savings Account (HSA), making it an optimal selection for employees seeking lower monthly payroll premiums and long-term tax-sheltered healthcare savings.

  • Annual Deductible: $1,500.00 USD for Individual coverage; $3,000.00 USD for Family coverage.
  • Coinsurance: In-network preventative care is covered at 100% ($0 cost). For all other diagnostic services, therapies, and hospitalizations, the plan covers 80% coinsurance after the annual deductible is satisfied.
  • Annual Out-of-Pocket Maximum: $4,000.00 USD Individual; $8,000.00 USD Family.
  • HSA Compatibility: Eligible for pre-tax employee payroll deductions and direct employer seed contributions.

2.3 The Standard PPO Plan

An intermediate value plan featuring a $1,000 Individual / $2,000 Family deductible with 80% in-network coinsurance and an out-of-pocket maximum of $3,500 Individual / $7,000 Family.

3. Health Savings Accounts (HSA) and Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

3.1 Employer-Funded Health Savings Account (HSA) Contributions

For employees who elect the HDHP medical option, GlobalTech provides a direct, non-taxable employer contribution deposited directly into the employee's Fidelity HSA:

  • Individual Coverage: $1,000.00 USD annual employer contribution.
  • Family Coverage (Employee + Spouse/Children): $2,000.00 USD annual employer contribution.
  • Contributions are deposited in two equal semi-annual disbursements on the first payroll cycle of January and July. Employees enrolled mid-year receive a prorated initial deposit.
  • Funds in an HSA belong 100% to the employee immediately, earn interest, can be invested in mutual funds/ETFs, roll over from year to year without forfeiture, and are completely portable if the employee leaves GlobalTech.

3.2 Healthcare Flexible Spending Account (FSA)

Employees enrolled in the Premier PPO, Standard PPO, or who waive medical coverage may participate in the Healthcare FSA administered via WEX. Employees may set aside up to the IRS annual limit of $3,200.00 USD in pre-tax payroll deductions to pay for qualified out-of-pocket medical, dental, and vision expenses.

Rollover Limits: In accordance with IRS regulations, up to $640.00 USD of unused Healthcare FSA funds remaining on December 31 will roll over into the subsequent plan year. Any remaining balance exceeding $640.00 is permanently forfeited under the federal "use-it-or-lose-it" rule.

3.3 Dependent Care FSA

All regular employees (regardless of medical plan election) may elect a Dependent Care FSA up to the statutory household maximum of $5,000.00 USD per year ($2,500 if married filing separately) to reimburse qualified child daycare, preschool, after-school care, or elder care expenses for dependents under age 13.

4. Dental and Vision Insurance Coverage

4.1 Dental Care (Delta Dental Premier Network)

Comprehensive oral healthcare administered through Delta Dental:

  • Preventative & Diagnostic: 100% coverage with zero deductible for semi-annual cleanings, comprehensive oral exams, bitewing X-rays, and topical fluoride treatments.
  • Basic Restorative Services: 80% coverage after a modest $50 annual individual deductible (fillings, root canals, periodontal scaling, extractions).
  • Major Restorative Services: 50% coverage (crowns, bridges, full and partial dentures, surgical implants).
  • Annual Benefit Maximum: $2,000.00 USD per covered member per calendar year.
  • Orthodontia: 50% lifetime coverage up to a $2,000.00 lifetime maximum for child and adult orthodontic braces and clear aligners.

4.2 Vision Care (VSP Choice Network)

Vision wellness and corrective eyewear coverage administered through VSP:

  • Annual Eye Exam: Covered in full every 12 months with a flat $10.00 copayment.
  • Prescription Lenses: Single vision, bifocal, and trifocal lenses covered in full every 12 months with a $25.00 materials copay. Polycarbonate lenses covered at 100% for dependent children.
  • Frames Retail Allowance: Up to a $200.00 USD retail frame allowance every 12 months, plus a 20% discount on any out-of-pocket costs exceeding $200.
  • Contact Lenses: $200.00 annual allowance in lieu of prescription eyeglass lenses and frames.

5. Mental Health Support and Lifestyle Wellness Stipend

5.1 Mental Health & Emotional Well-being (Modern Health)

GlobalTech is deeply committed to mental health parity and emotional wellness. Through our partnership with Modern Health, all employees and their eligible dependents receive:

  • 12 Free Confidential Therapy Sessions: Video or in-person sessions with licensed clinical psychologists, marriage and family therapists, or clinical social workers per calendar year, fully paid by GlobalTech.
  • 12 Free Professional Coaching Sessions: Targeted sessions with certified executive and life coaches focusing on stress management, leadership, career transitions, and parental well-being.
  • Digital Mental Wellness Library: 24/7 on-demand access to guided meditation, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) modules, sleep soundscapes, and live group community circles.

5.2 Annual Lifestyle & Wellness Subsidy ($600 USD)

All active regular employees are eligible for an annual $600.00 USD lifestyle and wellness subsidy ($50.00 per month) managed through the Forma platform. Reimbursable categories include:

  • Gym memberships, fitness studio classes (CrossFit, Pilates, Yoga, martial arts).
  • Home fitness streaming subscriptions (Peloton, Apple Fitness+, Strava, Zwift).
  • Mindfulness and mental health apps (Headspace, Calm, Waking Up).
  • Ergonomic personal athletic gear, running shoes, and fitness tracker smartwatches (Apple Watch, Garmin, Whoop).
  • Marathon, triathlon, and endurance event registration fees.

6. 401(k) Retirement Savings Plan & Corporate Match

GlobalTech sponsors an enterprise 401(k) retirement savings plan administered through Fidelity Investments, featuring both Traditional Pre-Tax and Roth 401(k) deferral options.

6.1 Corporate Matching Structure (Up to 5% Total Match)

GlobalTech provides an aggressive matching formula to accelerate long-term retirement wealth creation:

  • Tier 1: 100% dollar-for-dollar employer match on the first 4.0% of eligible compensation contributed by the employee.
  • Tier 2: 50% match on the next 2.0% of eligible compensation contributed by the employee.
  • Maximum Corporate Contribution: An employee contributing 6.0% of their base salary receives the maximum corporate match of 5.0% of eligible compensation.

6.2 Immediate 100% Vesting

All employer matching contributions are subject to 100% immediate vesting with zero vesting cliff. The moment matching funds are deposited into your Fidelity account, they are 100% your legal property.

6.3 Auto-Enrollment & Annual Escalation

New employees are automatically enrolled in the 401(k) plan thirty (30) days following hire at a 6.0% pre-tax contribution rate into a Vanguard Target Retirement Date Fund matching their projected retirement age. Employees may opt out or modify contribution percentages and fund allocations at any time via NetBenefits.fidelity.com.

7. Life, Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D), and Disability Insurance

7.1 Company-Paid Basic Life and AD&D

GlobalTech automatically provides all regular full-time employees with basic group term life and accidental death and dismemberment (AD&D) insurance through Prudential, fully paid by the company at zero cost to the employee. The benefit coverage is equal to two times (2x) the employee's annual base salary, up to a plan maximum of $500,000.00 USD.

7.2 Voluntary Supplemental Life Insurance

Employees may elect additional voluntary life insurance coverage for themselves (up to 5x annual salary, max $1,000,000) and eligible spouses/children at competitive group payroll deduction rates. Guarantee issue amounts apply during initial new hire enrollment.

7.3 Short-Term Disability (STD)

In the event of an extended illness, injury, surgery, or pregnancy recovery preventing work, GlobalTech provides 100% company-paid Short-Term Disability insurance. STD pays 60% of pre-disability base salary (up to a weekly maximum of $2,500 USD) following a seven (7) calendar day elimination period, payable for up to twenty-six (26) consecutive weeks.

7.4 Long-Term Disability (LTD)

For medical conditions disabling an employee beyond 180 consecutive days, company-paid Long-Term Disability insurance provides income replacement equal to 60% of pre-disability base salary up to a monthly maximum benefit of $10,000.00 USD until social security normal retirement age or medical recovery.
