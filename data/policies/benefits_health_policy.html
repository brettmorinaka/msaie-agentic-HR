GlobalTech Employee Health Benefits & Welfare Plan

Document ID: POL-BEN-2024 | Version: 3.2 | Effective: January 1, 2024

Audience: Regular full-time employees working 30+ hours/week and eligible dependents.

1. Enrollment Windows and Eligibility

New hires become eligible for health benefits on their first day of official employment (Day 1 coverage). New team members must finalize elections within 30 calendar days of hire.

The annual Open Enrollment window takes place every year from November 1 through November 20, with changes taking effect January 1 of the subsequent calendar year.

Mid-year changes are permitted exclusively upon experiencing a Qualifying Life Event (QLE)—such as marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a dependent child, or loss of existing coverage. QLE documentation must be submitted within 30 days of the qualifying event.

2. Medical Insurance Plans

GlobalTech offers two comprehensive medical plans administered through BlueCross BlueShield:

  • Premier PPO Plan: $500 individual / $1,000 family annual deductible. In-network preventative services are covered at 100%. In-network medical visits and procedures are covered at 90% coinsurance after deductible. Maximum out-of-pocket cap is $2,500 individual / $5,000 family.
  • High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family deductible with 80% in-network coinsurance. Enables pre-tax contributions to a triple-tax-advantaged Health Savings Account (HSA).

3. Health Savings Accounts (HSA) and Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

For employees enrolled in the HDHP, GlobalTech provides a direct annual employer HSA contribution of $1,000 for individual coverage and $2,000 for family coverage, deposited semi-annually in January and July.

Employees enrolled in the PPO plan may elect a Healthcare Flexible Spending Account (FSA) with an annual IRS contribution limit of up to $3,200. FSAs are subject to a $640 year-end rollover limit; any funds beyond $640 remaining on December 31 are forfeited.

Dependent Care FSA is available to all eligible employees up to $5,000 annually for eligible child and elder care expenses.

4. Dental and Vision Coverage

Dental Care (Delta Dental): Preventative cleanings and examinations covered at 100% twice per calendar year. Basic restorative care covered at 80%. Major orthodontia and restorative care covered at 50% up to an annual benefit maximum of $2,000 per member.

Vision Care (VSP): Annual comprehensive eye examination covered with a $10 copay. Prescription lenses covered in full every 12 months. Frames allowance provides up to $200 retail allowance annually plus 20% discount on remaining balance.

5. Mental Health and Wellness Stipend

GlobalTech provides all active employees with a $600 annual wellness subsidy ($50/month), reimbursable for gym memberships, fitness classes, mindfulness apps, ergonomics, or marathon registrations via the lifestyle platform.

Mental health support is provided via Modern Health: 12 free confidential therapy or coaching sessions per calendar year for employees and each eligible dependent.

6. 401(k) Retirement Savings & Employer Match

Employees are eligible to participate in the 401(k) retirement plan through Fidelity immediately upon hire. GlobalTech provides a dollar-for-dollar employer match up to the first 4% of eligible base compensation, plus a 50% match on the subsequent 2% (total maximum employer match of 5%).

Employer matching contributions feature 100% immediate vesting with zero vesting cliff.
