GlobalTech Employee Onboarding, IT Hardware & Equipment Lifecycle Policy

Document ID: POL-ONB-2024 | Version: 2.5 | Effective: January 1, 2024

Audience: All Newly Hired Employees, Hiring Managers, Department Leads, and People Partners

1. Onboarding Philosophy and Journey Overview

At GlobalTech, we consider the employee onboarding experience to be the foundational cornerstone of long-term career growth, professional engagement, and team velocity. A thoughtful, structured onboarding journey ensures that every new team member feels warmly welcomed, culturally embraced, technically equipped, and empowered to contribute meaningful impact from their very first week.

Our structured onboarding lifecycle spans four distinct phases: Pre-Boarding (Offer acceptance to Day 0), First Week Immersion (Days 1–5), Ramp-Up & Team Integration (Days 6–30), and Autonomous Impact (Days 31–90).

2. Pre-Boarding Protocols and Legal Compliance Verification

2.1 Pre-Employment Background & Reference Verification

All formal employment offers are contingent upon the successful completion of a comprehensive pre-employment background screening administered by our certified partner (Checkr). Screenings encompass identity verification, criminal history (subject to municipal Fair Chance legislation), prior employment verification, and educational degree confirmation.

2.2 Statutory Employment Eligibility (Form I-9) Mandatory Timeline

In accordance with United States federal immigration statutes, all newly hired personnel operating within the United States must satisfy Form I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification requirements:

  • Section 1 (Employee Attestation): Must be completed electronically via the Workday Onboarding portal no later than the first business day of active employment.
  • Section 2 (Physical Document Inspection): Must be physically examined and certified by an authorized GlobalTech representative or verified remote mobile notary within three (3) business days of the employee's official start date.
  • Failure to satisfy Form I-9 document verification within the federal 3-day statutory window mandates immediate suspension of work and potential employment termination under federal law.

2.3 Pre-Boarding Welcome Portal & Buddy Assignment

Ten (10) business days prior to an employee's start date, the People Operations team invites the new hire to the GlobalTech Welcome Hub, provisions their corporate email address, and assigns a dedicated **Onboarding Buddy**—an experienced peer outside the direct reporting chain who provides friendly, informal guidance regarding company norms, cultural traditions, and team workflows.

3. Corporate IT Hardware Standards & Provisioning Lifecycles

3.1 Standard Laptop Packages by Functional Role

GlobalTech IT Enterprise Logistics configures and ships customized hardware packages directly to the employee's designated residential address to arrive at least two (2) business days prior to Day 1:

  • Software Engineering & Data Science: 16-inch Apple MacBook Pro (Apple Silicon M3 Max, 36GB Unified Memory, 1TB High-Speed SSD) or enterprise Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 6 workstation running Ubuntu Linux LTS.
  • Product Management, Design & UX: 16-inch Apple MacBook Pro (Apple Silicon M3 Pro, 36GB Unified Memory, Liquid Retina XDR Display).
  • Sales, Marketing, HR, Finance & Operations: 14-inch Apple MacBook Pro (Apple Silicon M3, 18GB Unified Memory) or Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Ultrabook running Windows 11 Enterprise.

3.2 Standard Peripheral Bundle

Every newly provisioned computer package includes a complete enterprise accessory bundle:

  • Two (2) YubiKey 5C NFC hardware security keys for FIDO2/WebAuthn multi-factor authentication.
  • Anker USB-C multi-port docking hub with HDMI 2.1, Ethernet, and USB-A legacy ports.
  • 65W/140W GaN fast charger with reinforced braided USB-C power cables.
  • Protective padded corporate laptop travel sleeve and webcam privacy cover.

3.3 Corporate Hardware Refresh Cycle (36 Months)

All primary corporate laptops are enrolled in an automated thirty-six (36) month hardware refresh lifecycle. Upon reaching three years of continuous operational service, the employee is automatically prompted by the IT portal to select a next-generation replacement system. The legacy machine must be wiped and returned within fourteen (14) days of receiving the new workstation.

4. Home Office Ergonomic Allowance ($750 USD) Execution Details

Under the GlobalTech Remote & Hybrid Work Policy (POL-REMOTE-2024), newly approved fully remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office equipment allowance of up to $750.00 USD.

4.1 Permissible Ergonomic Expenditures

The stipend is dedicated strictly to physical ergonomic infrastructure that fosters workplace health and sustained productivity:

  • ANSI/BIFMA certified ergonomic office chairs (e.g., Herman Miller, Steelcase, Fully, Branch).
  • Electric motorized sit-to-stand desks or manual desktop sit-stand risers.
  • External computer monitors (up to two 27-inch 4K UHD displays or one 34-inch ultrawide monitor).
  • Ergonomic keyboard accessories, vertical mice, and pneumatic monitor desk mounting arms.
  • Anti-fatigue standing floor mats, footrests, and ergonomic desk task lighting.

4.2 Expense Submission Workflow in SAP Concur

To receive reimbursement, the employee must submit an expense report in SAP Concur:

  • Expense Category: Select *"Home Office Equipment Stipend"*.
  • Eligible Window: Expenses must be purchased and submitted within sixty (60) calendar days of the employee's formal start date or remote status approval.
  • Documentation: Upload clear, itemized merchant receipts showing date, vendor name, item description, and total paid.
  • Reimbursements are processed via direct payroll deposit within two (2) subsequent payroll cycles following manager approval.

5. First 30-60-90 Day Milestones & Performance Cadence

5.1 Day 1: Welcome & Orientation

  • Attend the virtual GlobalTech New Hire Welcome Orientation session with People Operations.
  • Complete Okta identity provisioning, YubiKey setup, and 1Password enterprise enrollment.
  • Review the Code of Business Ethics (POL-ETHICS-2024) and sign corporate compliance disclosures.
  • Initial 1-on-1 welcome sync with Hiring Manager and Onboarding Buddy.

5.2 Days 2–30: Foundation & Environment Setup

  • Clone repositories, configure local development Docker environments, and execute first code commit or documentation pull request.
  • Finalize medical, dental, and vision insurance elections in Workday within the 30-calendar-day deadline (POL-BEN-2024).
  • Complete required compliance training modules: Anti-Harassment in the Workplace, SOC 2 Security Awareness, and GDPR Data Handling.
  • Conduct formal 30-day check-in with manager to review initial onboarding roadmap progress.

5.3 Days 31–60: Team Integration & Feature Delivery

  • Assume ownership of standard sprint feature stories or core operational responsibilities.
  • Participate in agile sprint ceremonies, retrospectives, and cross-functional design reviews.
  • 60-day progress review with manager evaluating delivery quality and team collaboration.

5.4 Days 61–90: Autonomous Contribution & Probation Review

  • Deliver substantial independent contributions and participate in on-call shadow rotations if applicable.
  • Formal 90-day introductory probationary review: Manager and People Partner assess overall performance rating (must achieve "Meets Expectations" or higher to conclude introductory probation).

6. Hardware Return Protocols upon Employee Separation

All corporate hardware, laptops, monitors, security tokens, and corporate access badges remain the exclusive property of GlobalTech and must be surrendered upon separation of employment:

  • Prepaid Return Shipping Kit: Upon notice of resignation or termination, GlobalTech IT Logistics automatically dispatches a secure, pre-labeled, insured return shipping container to the employee's home address.
  • Return Timeline: The employee must pack all company-owned hardware and surrender the package to FedEx or UPS within five (5) business days of their final day of employment.
  • Data Sanitation: Laptops are remotely locked and cryptographically sanitized via MDM upon separation. Employees must not attempt to manually format drives or alter system firmware.
  • Failure to return company equipment within fourteen (14) days will result in legal recovery proceedings and reporting of unreturned assets to law enforcement authorities.
