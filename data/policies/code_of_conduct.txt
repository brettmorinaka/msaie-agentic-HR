GLOBALTECH CODE OF BUSINESS ETHICS AND WORKPLACE CONDUCT
Document ID: POL-ETHICS-2024
Category: Compliance & Ethics
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Audience: All GlobalTech Staff, Contractors, and Officers

1. PURPOSE AND CORE VALUES
GlobalTech is dedicated to fostering a professional, inclusive, safe, and respectful environment. Every employee, contractor, officer, and director is expected to uphold the highest ethical standards of integrity, transparency, and accountability in all business activities.

2. EQUAL OPPORTUNITY AND ANTI-HARASSMENT POLICY
GlobalTech maintains a zero-tolerance policy regarding discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or bullying in any form. Unlawful discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, pregnancy, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or genetic information is strictly prohibited.
- Prohibited harassment includes unwelcome verbal remarks, offensive slurs, derogatory jokes, unwelcome romantic advances, and visual displays of offensive material.
- Supervisors and managers who observe or receive reports of harassment are strictly required to report the matter to People Operations within 24 hours.

3. REPORTING PROCEDURES AND NON-RETALIATION GUARANTEE
Employees who experience or witness potential violations of this Code have multiple reporting channels:
1. Direct Supervisor or Department Head.
2. People Operations (HR) via email to ethics@globaltech-hr.internal or by filing a confidential HR ticket.
3. GlobalTech Ethics Helpline: An independent, anonymous 24/7 hotline available online at ethics.globaltech.internal or by phone at 1-800-555-GT-ETHIC.
GlobalTech guarantees that no employee will suffer retaliation, adverse employment action, demotion, or harassment as a result of making a good-faith report of unethical conduct or participating in an internal investigation.

4. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST AND SECONDARY EMPLOYMENT
Employees must avoid any interest, investment, or relationship that conflicts or appears to conflict with the best interests of GlobalTech.
- Moonlighting / Secondary Employment: Secondary employment or advisory roles are permitted only if they do not interfere with performance, do not use company property or confidential IP, and have received prior written sign-off from the Department Vice President and Chief Legal Officer.
- Employment with direct competitors or vendors providing services to GlobalTech is strictly prohibited.

5. GIFTS, ENTERTAINMENT, AND ANTI-BRIBERY
- Modest Gifts: Modest business meals and promotional items (e.g., logo merchandise) with an aggregate value under $75 USD per occasion may be accepted without prior disclosure.
- Prohibited Gifts: Cash or cash-equivalents (gift cards, prepaid debit cards) in any amount are strictly prohibited.
- Gifts or entertainment exceeding $75 USD must be declared in writing to the Corporate Compliance Officer for formal review.
- Bribery and Kickbacks: Providing or accepting anything of value to influence official action or commercial transactions violates the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and is cause for immediate termination with cause.

6. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Employees must preserve the confidentiality of proprietary information, trade secrets, algorithms, customer lists, and financial figures. This duty continues indefinitely following termination of employment.
