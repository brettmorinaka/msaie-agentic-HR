# GlobalTech Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-REMOTE-2024  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** March 15, 2024  
**Category:** Workplace Flexibility  
**Audience:** All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees  

---

## 1. Purpose and Philosophy
GlobalTech embraces a flexible, high-trust work culture that empowers team members to deliver exceptional results regardless of physical location. This policy outlines eligibility criteria, workspace standards, financial allowances, and operational requirements for hybrid and fully remote employees.

## 2. Eligibility Requirements
1. **Employment Status:** All regular full-time employees are eligible to apply for remote or hybrid status following the successful completion of their 90-day introductory probationary period.
2. **Performance Standing:** Employees must maintain a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher on their most recent quarterly performance review. Employees on an active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) are not eligible for fully remote arrangements and must maintain an on-site or hybrid schedule.
3. **Role Compatibility:** Role eligibility is determined by Department Leadership. Roles requiring physical equipment interaction, on-site facilities management, or direct hardware testing are classified as "On-site Essential".

## 3. Home Office Equipment Allowance & Expenses
1. **Initial Equipment Allowance:** Newly approved fully remote and hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time home office equipment stipend of up to **$750 USD**. Eligible purchases include ergonomic chairs, sit-stand desk convertors, monitors, and keyboard/mouse accessories. Purchases must be made within 60 days of remote approval and submitted via Concur with itemized receipts.
2. **Monthly Internet & Utility Subsidy:** Remote employees receive a monthly non-taxable stipend of **$50 USD** automatically included in their regular payroll cycle to offset broadband internet costs.
3. **Company Hardware:** GlobalTech provides one standard laptop (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad), one monitor, and essential peripherals. All company hardware remains the property of GlobalTech and must be returned upon separation.

## 4. Working Hours, Availability, and Communication
1. **Core Collaboration Hours:** Regardless of individual time zones, employees must maintain availability for synchronous collaboration during core hours from **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their designated team time zone**.
2. **Status and Responsiveness:** Remote employees are expected to maintain an up-to-date calendar, utilize Slack status indicators for focused work or lunch breaks, and acknowledge urgent team communications within 2 hours during core working hours.

## 5. Temporary International Remote Work ("Workation")
1. **Annual Limit:** Employees in good standing may work remotely from an approved international location for up to **30 calendar days per calendar year**.
2. **Notice & Approval Procedure:** Requests must be formally submitted through the HR Portal at least **4 weeks (28 days) in advance** to allow Legal, Tax, and Information Security reviews.
3. **Restricted Jurisdictions:** Remote work is strictly prohibited from jurisdictions subject to comprehensive United States trade embargoes or sanctions (including Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and occupied regions of Ukraine).
4. **Expense Disallowance:** All travel, lodging, visa fees, and personal meals during international workations are personal expenses and are **strictly non-reimbursable** under GlobalTech's Expense and Travel Policy.
5. **Tax & Legal Liability:** The employee remains personally responsible for complying with local visa regulations, ensuring valid work authorization, and resolving any personal tax liabilities.

## 6. Information Security and Workspace Privacy
1. **VPN Requirement:** All remote devices must connect to the GlobalTech Enterprise VPN whenever accessing internal repositories, customer data, or staging environments.
2. **Device Security:** Screens must be locked when unattended (auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity). Working from public networks without VPN or in spaces where confidential information is visible to unauthorized parties is a violation of the Code of Conduct.
