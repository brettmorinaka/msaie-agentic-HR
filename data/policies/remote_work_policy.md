**Last Revised:** March 15, 2024

**Policy Owner:** People Operations & Global Workplace Experience

## 1. Purpose and Philosophy
GlobalTech embraces a flexible, high-trust, and results-driven work culture that empowers team members to deliver exceptional impact regardless of their physical work location. We recognize that modern software development, collaborative engineering, and global corporate operations flourish when employees are afforded autonomy over their working environments, balanced with clear responsibilities regarding communication, data security, team cohesion, and regulatory compliance.

This comprehensive policy establishes the binding guidelines, eligibility frameworks, workspace safety standards, financial stipends, tax obligations, and operational expectations governing all flexible work arrangements at GlobalTech. Flexible work arrangements encompass three primary work modalities:
1. **Fully Remote:** The employee works 100% of their time from an approved home office or co-working space located within their home country, attending in-person office gatherings only for periodic team summits or company-wide kickoffs.
2. **Hybrid Work:** The employee splits their weekly schedule between an official GlobalTech physical office facility (minimum 2 designated anchor days per week) and an approved remote home office.
3. **Office-Centric:** The employee operates predominantly (4 to 5 days per week) from an assigned GlobalTech corporate office or engineering hub.

## 2. Eligibility Requirements & Performance Standards
Flexible working arrangements are a privilege and a collaborative operational agreement between the employee, their department manager, and People Operations. Eligibility is evaluated against the following mandatory criteria:

### 2.1 Introductory Probationary Period
All newly hired regular full-time and part-time employees are subject to an initial ninety-day introductory probationary period (**90-day probationary period**). 
- During this initial 90-day period, newly onboarded staff are expected to work in close alignment with their onboarding buddy, team lead, and designated manager to ensure comprehensive immersion into GlobalTech engineering architectures, business workflows, and cultural expectations.
- Employees hired into office-centric or hybrid roles are not eligible to transition to a fully remote arrangement until they have formally completed their 90-day introductory review with an explicit sign-off from their People Partner.
- Direct-hire fully remote roles (expressly designated as remote in the initial employment agreement) are exempt from the physical office requirement during probation, but must undergo rigorous 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day check-ins evaluating remote autonomy, delivery velocity, and asynchronous communication proficiency.

### 2.2 Performance Standing and Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)
Continued eligibility for remote and hybrid status requires sustained strong performance:
- Employees must maintain a performance rating of **"Meets Expectations"** (Level 3 or higher on our 5-point performance scale) on their most recent quarterly review and annual performance assessment.
- **Performance Improvement Plans (PIP):** An employee placed on an active formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) or written disciplinary remediation is deemed temporarily ineligible for fully remote working arrangements. At the discretion of the Department Vice President and People Partner, the employee may be required to transition to an on-site or closely supervised hybrid schedule for the duration of the 30-day to 60-day remediation cycle to facilitate intensive mentoring and daily standup check-ins.
- If performance returns to a sustained satisfactory level following successful completion of the PIP, the employee may re-apply for remote status with manager and HR endorsement.

### 2.3 Role Compatibility & On-Site Essential Classifications
Not all organizational functions can be performed remotely with equivalent operational efficacy. Department leadership maintains an updated roster of job families classified as "On-Site Essential":
- Facilities management, workplace services, physical office reception, and mail operations.
- Hardware engineering requiring clean rooms, oscilloscopes, physical soldering, or specialized on-premise lab test rigs.
- On-premise enterprise data center infrastructure maintenance and physical server provisioning.
- Roles designated as On-Site Essential are ineligible for fully remote work, though managers may authorize ad-hoc remote days during severe inclement weather or facility maintenance closures.

## 3. Home Office Equipment Allowance & Monthly Stipends

### 3.1 Initial Home Office Equipment Allowance ($750 USD)
GlobalTech is committed to ensuring that all approved remote and hybrid team members operate within ergonomically sound, healthy, and highly productive home environments.
- **Allowance Amount:** Newly approved fully remote and hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time reimbursement stipend of up to **$750.00 USD** (or local currency equivalent based on current corporate exchange rates).
- **Eligible Items:** Reimbursable expenditures under this one-time stipend include:
  1. Ergonomic task chairs certified by ANSI/BIFMA standards.
  2. Sit-stand motorized desks or manual desk converters.
  3. External monitors (up to two 27-inch 4K displays or one ultrawide monitor).
  4. Ergonomic keyboards, vertical mice, trackpads, and monitor mounting arms.
  5. Blue-light filtering desk lamps, laptop risers, and anti-fatigue floor mats.
  6. Noise-canceling video conferencing headsets or external desktop microphones.
- **Ineligible Items:** Decorative furniture, home renovations, soundproofing construction, air conditioning units, gaming consoles, televisions, and personal tablet computers are strictly non-reimbursable.
- **Submission Timeline:** Expenses must be incurred within **60 calendar days** of the employee's formal remote status approval date. Claims must be submitted via the Concur expense system under the category *"Home Office Equipment Stipend"* accompanied by itemized merchant receipts showing date, item description, and payment confirmation.
- **Ownership:** Equipment purchased through this stipend becomes the personal property of the employee; however, employees who voluntarily resign from GlobalTech within six (6) months of receiving the stipend are required to repay 50% of the reimbursed sum via deduction from their final payroll disbursement where permitted by law.

### 3.2 Monthly Internet & Utility Subsidy ($50 USD)
- Active remote employees receive a recurring, non-taxable monthly internet and utility stipend of **$50.00 USD** (or local currency equivalent) disbursed automatically via standard payroll cycles.
- This stipend is designed to defray high-speed residential fiber/broadband subscription costs and home electricity usage required for daily corporate operations.
- Remote employees are required to maintain a dedicated, reliable residential broadband connection delivering a minimum download speed of **50 Mbps** and upload speed of **15 Mbps**, with latency below 40 ms, capable of supporting uninterrupted high-definition video conferencing and large git repository synchronization.
- Cellular mobile hotspots are considered emergency backup connections only and cannot serve as the primary residential broadband connection.

### 3.3 Corporate Hardware Provisioning & Refresh Cycles
- In addition to the equipment stipend, GlobalTech IT Enterprise Operations provides every employee with standardized enterprise hardware:
  - Engineering & Product: 16-inch Apple MacBook Pro (Apple Silicon M-series, 36GB unified memory) or high-spec Lenovo ThinkPad P-series Linux workstation.
  - Business Operations & Sales: 14-inch Apple MacBook Pro or Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon.
  - Standard peripherals: YubiKey 5-series multi-factor authentication hardware keys, USB-C multi-port adapter, and enterprise laptop sleeve.
- Hardware is automatically scheduled for a corporate refresh every **36 months**.
- All corporate computers remain the strict intellectual property of GlobalTech. In the event of separation, hardware must be returned via prepaid IT shipping containers within five (5) business days.

## 4. Working Hours, Availability, and Communication Etiquette

### 4.1 Core Collaboration Hours
To preserve the agility of cross-functional agile squads across distributed geographical regions, GlobalTech enforces standardized core collaboration hours:
- Regardless of personal residential time zones, all full-time employees must maintain active availability for synchronous meetings, pair programming, and operational standups between **10:00 AM and 4:00 PM** in their team's designated primary regional time zone (e.g., Pacific Time for West Coast engineering teams; Eastern Time for East Coast operations).
- Outside of core collaboration hours, employees are encouraged to structure their working blocks flexibly to support deep focus time, child care, physical health, and family commitments.
- Part-time employees must establish their specific weekly availability schedules with their manager in writing.

### 4.2 Status Transparency & Communication SLA
Effective remote work relies upon proactive communication and asynchronous transparency:
- **Slack Status Indicators:** Employees must actively update their Slack status throughout the workday:
  - 🟢 Active / Available
  - 📅 In a Meeting / Call
  - 🎯 Focus Time / Deep Work (snooze notifications enabled)
  - 🍽️ Lunch / Stepped Away (expected return time indicated)
  - 🌴 Out of Office / Vacation (matching approved HR PTO calendar dates)
- **Response SLAs:** During core collaboration hours, team members are expected to acknowledge direct Slack mentions or urgent operational escalations within **two (2) business hours**. For non-urgent email inquiries, a 24-hour response window is standard.
- **Calendar Visibility:** All employees must maintain transparent Google Calendar availability. Focus blocks, personal stepping-away appointments, and meetings must be clearly designated. Working hours must be explicitly configured in calendar settings.

### 4.3 Meeting Etiquette & Video Conferencing
- Default to "Camera-On" during team sprint retrospectives, 1-on-1 check-ins, interview panels, and company-wide all-hands meetings to foster human connection and non-verbal rapport.
- Mute microphones by default when not speaking in group sessions of five or more attendees.
- Avoid multi-tasking, answering unrelated Slack messages, or working on code reviews while actively participating in collaborative discussions.

## 5. Temporary International Remote Work ("Workation Policy")

GlobalTech recognizes that team members may occasionally wish to combine personal travel or extended family visits abroad with temporary remote work. We support international remote work opportunities within strict legal, regulatory, and corporate tax boundaries.

### 5.1 Annual Cap: 30 Calendar Days Per Year
- Eligible regular full-time employees in good standing who have completed their 90-day probationary period may apply to work remotely from an approved international country for up to **thirty (30) calendar days per calendar year**.
- The 30-day allocation is calculated on a rolling calendar-year basis (January 1 to December 31) and represents an aggregate limit across all international trips within that year.
- Unused international workation days do not roll over into subsequent calendar years and have zero cash-out value.
- Days taken as personal PTO during an international trip do not count against the 30-day remote work limit, but total continuous presence in a foreign jurisdiction (remote work plus PTO) must not exceed forty-five (45) consecutive days to prevent permanent establishment tax risks.

### 5.2 Mandatory Notice & Approval Protocol (4 Weeks / 28 Days in Advance)
- Due to international corporate tax treaties, permanent establishment risks, export control regulations, and European Union GDPR data transfer restrictions, all international remote work requests must be submitted at least **four (4) weeks (28 calendar days) in advance** through the HR Self-Service Portal.
- The approval workflow requires automated sequential sign-offs from:
  1. Direct Department Manager (assessing team sprint coverage and time zone overlap).
  2. People Operations (verifying employee tenure, performance standing, and remaining 30-day balance).
  3. Corporate Legal & Tax Compliance (verifying local labor laws and corporate tax exposure).
  4. Global Information Security (verifying cryptographic device compliance and travel risk profiles).
- Requests submitted fewer than 14 days prior to departure will be automatically denied, except in cases of verified family bereavement or humanitarian emergencies.

### 5.3 Prohibited & Sanctioned Jurisdictions
Remote work is strictly prohibited from jurisdictions where United States or European Union economic trade sanctions, export administration regulations (EAR), or elevated geopolitical travel advisories apply:
- **Embargoed Nations:** Absolutely zero corporate work, network connectivity, or device transport is permitted in Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and the occupied regions of Ukraine (Crimea, Donetsk, and Luhansk regions).
- **High-Risk Jurisdictions:** Remote work from the Russian Federation, Belarus, and specific State Department Level 4 ("Do Not Travel") conflict zones is strictly disallowed under corporate security mandates.
- Attempting to connect to GlobalTech VPN or SaaS systems from prohibited jurisdictions will trigger an automated security alert, immediate revocation of corporate credentials, and referral to Legal and Internal Audit for disciplinary action up to and including termination.

### 5.4 Strict Disallowance of Travel Expenses During Workations
- Under both this policy and the GlobalTech Travel & Expense Policy (**POL-EXP-2024**, Section 6), international workations are undertaken entirely at the employee's personal choice and expense.
- **Strictly Non-Reimbursable:** International airfare, trains, lodging, rental vehicles, personal dining, per diems, visa fees, tourist taxes, international roaming charges, and co-working day passes incurred during personal workations are **100% personal expenses**.
- Employees are strictly prohibited from expensing any personal travel, lodging, or meal costs in Concur associated with a workation. Submitting personal travel or meal receipts for corporate reimbursement under the guise of business travel is deemed serious financial misconduct and grounds for immediate termination.

### 5.5 Employee Legal, Immigration, and Tax Obligations
- The employee bears sole responsibility for verifying and complying with immigration, visa, and passport regulations for their destination country.
- Working on a standard tourist visa may violate local immigration laws in certain jurisdictions; employees must secure appropriate Digital Nomad visas, Schengen business allowances, or bilateral work permits.
- GlobalTech does not provide personal immigration sponsorship or tax counseling for temporary personal workations. Any local personal income tax withholdings or individual tax returns triggered by the employee's physical presence abroad remain the exclusive responsibility of the employee.

## 6. Information Security, Data Privacy, and Workspace Hygiene

### 6.1 Enterprise VPN & Zero-Trust Architecture
- Every remote device transmitting corporate traffic, accessing source code repositories (GitHub Enterprise), communicating via Slack, or querying production/staging databases must run the GlobalTech Cloudflare Zero Trust / WireGuard corporate VPN client.
- Split-tunneling is strictly disabled for internal enterprise routes.
- Connecting to public, unencrypted Wi-Fi networks (such as hotel lobbies, airports, cafes, or public transit) without an active corporate VPN connection is a direct violation of company security standards.

### 6.2 Physical Workspace Privacy & Clean Desk Guidelines
- Remote workers must ensure that confidential company data, client intellectual property, and proprietary algorithms cannot be viewed by unauthorized third parties, family members, or co-inhabitants.
- Employees must position their monitors away from windows, public walkways, and common household areas, or utilize privacy screen filters.
- **Automatic Screen Locking:** All corporate laptops are configured via MDM to automatically lock displays after five (5) minutes of user inactivity. Employees must manually lock screens (`Cmd + Ctrl + Q` on macOS, `Win + L` on Windows) whenever stepping away from their workspace.
- Hard-copy documents containing personally identifiable information (PII) or confidential financial data must be stored in locked file cabinets and disposed of using cross-cut shredders.

## 7. Ergonomics, Health, Safety, and Worker's Compensation

### 7.1 Home Ergonomic Assessments
- GlobalTech provides all remote team members with access to our virtual ergonomics platform (ErgoCare) to complete a personalized self-assessment within thirty (30) days of transitioning to remote status.
- Employees should adjust chair height such that feet rest flat on the floor or a footrest, thighs are parallel to the ground, and forearms rest comfortably at desk level with wrists in a neutral position.
- Computer displays should be positioned at approximately an arm's length distance, with the top third of the screen aligned at eye level.

### 7.2 Reporting Workplace Injuries at Home
- Remote employees are covered by GlobalTech Worker's Compensation insurance for bona fide occupational injuries sustained during designated working hours while performing assigned work duties within their designated home office area.
- Injuries sustained outside of the designated home office area (e.g., in kitchens, garages, yards, or while performing personal errands) are not covered under occupational worker's compensation.
- Any injury sustained during the course of work must be formally reported to People Operations and the Employee Safety Committee within **twenty-four (24) hours** using the online Incident Report form.

## 8. Revocation, Modifications, and Transition Protocols
- Remote and hybrid work arrangements are subject to ongoing operational review. GlobalTech reserves the right to modify, suspend, or revoke remote work privileges based on business restructuring, team re-alignment, or individual performance degradation.
- Except in instances of gross disciplinary misconduct or urgent business emergencies, managers will provide employees with a minimum of **thirty (30) calendar days advance written notice** if an operational requirement necessitates transitioning from fully remote to hybrid or office-centric status.
- Employees seeking to voluntarily relocate their primary permanent residence to a different state, province, or country must submit a formal Relocation Request to People Operations at least sixty (60) days in advance to verify corporate tax registrations, compensation tier adjustments, and local employment law compliance.
