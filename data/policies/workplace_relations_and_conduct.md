# GlobalTech Workplace Relationships, Performance Management & Conduct Policy

**Document ID:** POL-REL-2024  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** March 1, 2024  
**Category:** Employee Relations & Workplace Conduct  
**Audience:** All GlobalTech Employees, Managers, Directors, and Executive Leadership  
**Policy Owner:** People Operations & Employee Relations Committee  

---

## 1. Purpose and Philosophy
GlobalTech is dedicated to maintaining a high-performance, respectful, and ethically transparent professional working environment. We recognize that in a modern collaborative enterprise, close collegial friendships and consensual personal relationships naturally develop. At the same time, the company has an absolute obligation to prevent conflicts of interest, perceived or actual favoritism, sexual harassment, and disruptions to team morale.

This policy outlines the governing standards regarding personal and familial relationships in the workplace, our structured performance management and review framework, formal Performance Improvement Plans (PIP), professional conduct and attire expectations, and our substance-free workplace commitments.

---

## 2. Consensual Personal Relationships & Anti-Nepotism Standards

### 2.1 Consensual Romantic and Dating Relationships
GlobalTech respects the private lives of its employees and does not prohibit consensual romantic or dating relationships between co-workers, provided that such relationships do not create actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest, favoritism, or a hostile work environment.

### 2.2 Strict Prohibition on Direct or Indirect Supervisory Reporting
- **Prohibited Hierarchy:** A direct or indirect supervisory reporting relationship between individuals involved in a romantic, dating, sexual, or domestic partnership is **strictly prohibited**.
- No employee may manage, directly supervise, evaluate performance for, recommend compensation increases for, assign work to, or influence promotion decisions regarding an individual with whom they are romantically involved.
- This prohibition extends across all levels of the organization and applies equally to peer relationships where one party has functional oversight or budgetary audit authority over the other (e.g., an internal auditor dating an engineering lead whose budget they audit).

### 2.3 Mandatory Disclosure Obligation (Within 14 Calendar Days)
- Whenever a consensual romantic, dating, or intimate relationship begins between employees where one party has supervisory or influential authority over the other, both individuals are strictly required to disclose the relationship in writing to People Operations within **fourteen (14) calendar days of relationship inception**.
- **Confidential Management Plan:** People Operations will collaborate confidentially with the involved parties and department leadership to execute a conflict remediation plan:
  1. Reassigning direct reporting lines to an alternate department manager.
  2. Transferring one of the individuals to an open equivalent role within a separate team or department without reduction in base pay or rank.
  3. Establishing third-party compensation and performance review mechanisms to eliminate any appearance of bias.
- Failure to disclose a romantic relationship involving a supervisory or influential dynamic within the mandatory 14-day window is a serious disciplinary violation that may result in immediate suspension, termination of managerial status, or employment separation.

### 2.4 Employment of Relatives (Anti-Nepotism Policy)
- GlobalTech welcomes qualified referrals and applications from family members of current staff; however, relatives (spouses, children, siblings, parents, in-laws, cousins, domestic partners) may not work in the same direct line of reporting.
- Relatives may not participate in interview assessment panels, hiring decision committees, or promotion reviews for family members.

## 3. Performance Management, Feedback Cadence & Evaluation Cycles

GlobalTech believes that continuous, constructive, and bidirectional feedback drives engineering excellence and career fulfillment.

### 3.1 Semi-Annual Performance Evaluation Cadence
- **Mid-Year Check-in (July):** Focuses on progress against annual Objectives and Key Results (OKRs), core competencies, professional development goals, and 360-degree peer feedback.
- **Annual Year-End Review (December / January):** Comprehensive evaluation assessing overall business impact, architectural contributions, leadership behaviors, and final annual performance ratings that determine merit salary adjustments, annual bonus multipliers, and equity refresh grants.

### 3.2 Five-Point Performance Rating Scale
Performance evaluations utilize a standardized, company-wide 5-point performance scale:
- **Level 5 (Exceptional Impact):** Consistently exceeds role expectations across all dimensions; drives transformative organizational outcomes and mentors others broadly.
- **Level 4 (Exceeds Expectations):** Frequently delivers results above role requirements; displays high technical mastery and strong cross-functional leadership.
- **Level 3 (Meets Expectations):** Fully proficient; reliably hits project sprint deliverables, exhibits solid collaboration, and meets all core performance metrics.
- **Level 2 (Needs Improvement):** Performance is inconsistent; misses critical deadlines, requires heavy supervisory intervention, or exhibits gaps in core technical execution.
- **Level 1 (Unacceptable Performance):** Consistently fails to meet essential job requirements, lacks basic role competencies, or exhibits severe behavioral deficiencies.

## 4. Formal Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)

When an employee exhibits sustained performance deficits or receives a Level 2 or Level 1 rating, management may initiate a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) to provide structured remediation.

### 4.1 PIP Structure and Duration (30 to 60 Days)
- A PIP is a formal written document co-authored by the Hiring Manager and the People Partner.
- **Core Components:**
  1. Detailed documentation of specific performance deficiencies with objective examples.
  2. Concrete, measurable success criteria, milestone deliverables, and expected behavioral standards.
  3. Clearly defined weekly check-in schedules between the employee and manager.
  4. Explicit timeline: Standard PIP duration is **thirty (30) to sixty (60) calendar days**.
  5. Clear consequences: Explicit statement that failure to meet and sustain all required standards by the conclusion of the PIP will result in employment termination.

### 4.2 Impact of PIP on Remote Work and Internal Mobility
- **Remote Work Ineligibility:** As established in **POL-REMOTE-2024** (Section 2.2), an employee on an active formal PIP is temporarily ineligible for fully remote work and may be required to maintain an on-site or hybrid schedule to facilitate intensive daily mentoring.
- **Internal Transfer Freeze:** Employees on an active PIP are barred from applying for internal job transfers, lateral team moves, or promotional advancements until they have successfully completed the PIP and maintained a "Meets Expectations" rating for at least six (6) subsequent months.

## 5. Professional Attire, Workplace Grooming & Video Conference Etiquette

### 5.1 Smart Casual Attire Standards
- GlobalTech maintains a **"Smart Casual"** attire standard across all physical corporate campuses and engineering hubs. Employees should dress comfortably and neatly. Clean jeans, collared shirts, blouses, sweaters, clean sneakers, and casual dresses are welcome.
- Inappropriate office attire includes clothing featuring offensive slogans, vulgar graphics, ripped or transparent garments, or footwear that violates lab safety regulations.
- When representing GlobalTech at external client presentations, executive board meetings, investor roadshows, or formal industry conferences, business casual or business professional attire is expected.

### 5.2 Virtual Video Conference Etiquette
- During remote video conferences, employees must dress appropriately in clean casual attire suitable for an office environment.
- Virtual backgrounds or blurred background filters should be utilized if the physical home workspace contains visual clutter, personal family items, or non-work distractions.

## 6. Substance-Free Workplace & Prohibited Substances

GlobalTech is dedicated to maintaining a safe, healthy, productive, and drug-free work environment for all employees, visitors, and partners.

### 6.1 Prohibited Conduct
- The unlawful manufacture, distribution, dispensing, possession, sale, purchase, or use of illegal controlled substances, narcotics, or unauthorized prescription drugs on company premises, in company vehicles, or while performing company business is strictly forbidden.
- Reporting to work or conducting corporate business while impaired by alcohol, cannabis, or illegal drugs is zero-tolerance misconduct.
- Reasonable Suspicion Testing: Where permitted by applicable law, GlobalTech reserves the right to require an employee to undergo an independent medical drug/alcohol test if observable symptoms or objective evidence indicates on-the-job impairment.

### 6.2 Authorized Social Events
- Moderate, lawful consumption of beer and wine is permitted at designated company-sponsored celebrations (e.g., product launches, holiday parties) under the supervision of corporate event managers. Non-alcoholic beverage alternatives must always be prominently provided.
- Employees who choose to consume alcohol at company events are expected to exercise personal responsibility, moderation, and ensure safe transportation.
