# GlobalTech Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave of Absence Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PTO-2024  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** February 10, 2024  
**Category:** Time Off & Benefits  
**Audience:** All Regular Full-Time and Part-Time Employees  

---

## 1. Overview
GlobalTech recognizes the paramount importance of rest, personal well-being, and work-life harmony. This policy establishes accrual guidelines, advance notice requirements, rollover limits, and statutory leave categories.

## 2. Annual PTO Accrual Tiers
Paid Time Off (PTO) accrues semi-monthly on each pay period (1st and 15th of the month) based on continuous employee tenure:
- **Tier 1 (Tenure 0 to 2 years):** Accrues at 5.0 hours per pay period, totaling **15 days (120 hours)** per full calendar year.
- **Tier 2 (Tenure 2+ to 5 years):** Accrues at 6.67 hours per pay period, totaling **20 days (160 hours)** per full calendar year.
- **Tier 3 (Tenure 5+ years):** Accrues at 8.33 hours per pay period, totaling **25 days (200 hours)** per full calendar year.

Part-time employees working 20+ hours per week accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis.

## 3. PTO Request and Advance Notice Deadlines
To ensure team operational continuity and adequate coverage, employees must submit PTO requests through the HR Self-Service Portal in accordance with the following advance notice schedule:
1. **Single Day or Two-Day Requests (1–2 days):** Minimum **48 hours advance notice** required to immediate manager.
2. **Short Leaves (3–5 consecutive business days):** Minimum **2 weeks (14 days) advance notice** required.
3. **Extended Leaves (6 or more consecutive business days):** Minimum **30 calendar days advance notice** required, subject to written manager approval.
4. **Emergency Unscheduled Time Off:** In cases of unforeseen personal emergencies, employees must notify their manager and log the absence by 9:00 AM local time on the day of absence.

## 4. Year-End Rollover and Expiration
1. **Rollover Allowance:** Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days (40 hours)** into the subsequent calendar year.
2. **Rollover Expiration Deadline:** Any carried-over PTO days must be scheduled and taken prior to **March 31** of the new year, or they will be forfeited. Cash payouts for unused PTO are prohibited except where mandated by local state legislation (e.g., California, Colorado).
3. **Maximum Balance Cap:** Once an employee reaches a maximum accrual cap equal to 1.5 times their annual tier allowance, PTO accrual pauses until the balance falls below the cap.

## 5. Sick and Medical Leave
1. **Separate Sick Days:** Regular full-time employees receive **10 paid sick days (80 hours)** on January 1 of each year, frontloaded separately from general PTO.
2. **Medical Documentation:** A physician's or licensed medical provider's note is required for any consecutive sick leave absence exceeding **3 consecutive business days**.

## 6. Parental Leave
1. **Primary Caregiver:** Eligible full-time employees with at least 6 months of continuous service receive **16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave** following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
2. **Secondary Caregiver:** Eligible employees receive **8 weeks of 100% paid parental leave**.
3. **Usage Window:** Parental leave must be taken within 12 months following the qualifying birth or placement event and may be taken continuously or in two discrete blocks.

## 7. Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to paid bereavement leave as follows:
- **Immediate Family (Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling):** Up to **5 consecutive business days**.
- **Extended Family (Grandparent, In-law, Aunt/Uncle):** Up to **3 consecutive business days**.

## 8. Departmental Blackout Periods
Operational blackout windows apply during critical business cycles:
- **Finance & Accounting:** Last 5 business days of each fiscal quarter and first 10 business days of Q1 (annual audit).
- **Sales & Customer Success:** Final 7 business days of Q4.
Exceptions during blackout periods require written sign-off from the Department Vice President and Chief People Officer.
