## 1. Purpose and Guiding Philosophy
At GlobalTech, we recognize that sustained high performance, innovative thinking, and creative technical problem-solving require deliberate periods of rest, relaxation, personal exploration, and quality time with loved ones. We believe that vacations and personal time off rejuvenate the mind, prevent burnout, and foster sustainable long-term careers.

This comprehensive policy establishes the governing framework for all forms of time away from work, including accrued Paid Time Off (PTO), dedicated sick and medical leave, paid parental leave, bereavement leave, civic service leave, statutory family medical leaves, and extended personal sabbaticals.

## 2. Annual PTO Accrual Tiers and Calculations
Paid Time Off (PTO) at GlobalTech is an accrued benefit awarded on a semi-monthly basis across our standard 24 annual payroll cycles (on the 1st and 15th of each calendar month). Accrual rates are determined strictly by continuous full-time service tenure:

### 2.1 Accrual Schedule by Continuous Service Tenure
| Tenure Tier | Continuous Years of Service | Semi-Monthly Accrual Rate | Total Annual Accrual (Hours) | Total Annual Days (8-Hour Equivalent) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **0 to 2 years** (1 to 24 months) | **5.00 hours** / pay period | **120.0 hours** / year | **15.0 days** / year |
| **Tier 2** | **2+ to 5 years** (25 to 60 months) | **6.67 hours** / pay period | **160.0 hours** / year | **20.0 days** / year |
| **Tier 3** | **5+ years** (61+ months) | **8.33 hours** / pay period | **200.0 hours** / year | **25.0 days** / year |

### 2.2 Accrual Rules & Proration
- **Mid-Year Service Milestones:** An employee transitioning from Tier 1 to Tier 2 on their 2nd employment anniversary automatically begins accruing at the elevated 6.67 hours per pay period beginning on the first pay cycle immediately following the anniversary date.
- **Part-Time Employees:** Regular part-time employees scheduled to work twenty (20) or more hours per week accrue PTO on a strict pro-rata basis proportional to their authorized weekly hours relative to a 40-hour full-time workweek (e.g., a 20-hour/week employee in Tier 1 accrues 2.5 hours per pay period).
- **Ineligible Classifications:** Temporary workers, co-op interns, seasonal staff, and independent 1099 contractors do not accrue paid time off.
- **Accrual During Leaves:** PTO continues to accrue during paid leaves of absence (such as paid sick leave, paid parental leave, and bereavement leave). PTO accrual pauses during unpaid personal leaves of absence exceeding thirty (30) consecutive calendar days.

## 3. PTO Request Workflows and Mandatory Advance Notice Deadlines
To ensure uninterrupted operational service delivery, maintain engineering sprint cadence, and prevent undue coverage burdens on teammates, all planned PTO must be formally requested and approved in advance through the HR Self-Service Portal.

### 3.1 Advance Notice Requirements
1. **Single Day or Two-Day Requests (1–2 business days):**
   - Minimum **forty-eight (48) hours advance notice** required to the immediate team lead or engineering manager.
   - Designed for personal appointments, long weekends, or routine personal errands.
2. **Short Vacation Leaves (3–5 consecutive business days):**
   - Minimum **two (2) full weeks (14 calendar days) advance notice** required.
   - Allows sprint planning engineers to reallocate story points and designate emergency on-call backup.
3. **Extended Vacations (6 or more consecutive business days):**
   - Minimum **thirty (30) calendar days advance notice** required.
   - Requires formal written sign-off from the Department Director to ensure cross-functional coverage.
4. **Emergency / Unscheduled Absences:**
   - In instances of sudden personal emergencies, severe weather, or unexpected domestic crises, employees must notify their immediate manager via Slack or phone and record the absence in the HR portal no later than **9:00 AM local time** on the morning of the absence.

### 3.2 Manager Approval Discretion
- Managers evaluate PTO requests based on chronological submission order, project milestone deadlines, client deliverables, and team redundancy.
- Managers may not unreasonably withhold or deny PTO requests; however, if multiple engineers in the same operational pod request the identical week off, the manager may request staggering based on business necessity.

## 4. Year-End Rollover, Expiration Deadlines, and Maximum Accrual Caps

### 4.1 Rollover Allowance (Maximum 5 Days / 40 Hours)
- GlobalTech strongly encourages employees to take all accrued vacation days within the calendar year in which they are earned.
- As an accommodation, employees may carry over a maximum of **five (5) unused PTO days (forty [40] hours)** into the subsequent calendar year.
- Any accrued, unused PTO in excess of 5 days remaining on December 31 at 11:59 PM local time will be automatically forfeited, except where prohibited by applicable state law.

### 4.2 Rollover Expiration Deadline: March 31
- Carried-over rollover days are tracked separately in the HR portal under the balance bucket *"Prior Year Rollover"*.
- All rolled-over PTO days must be scheduled and taken prior to **March 31 of the new calendar year**.
- Any carried-over days that remain unused after March 31 will expire and be permanently removed from the employee's active balance.
- **Interaction with Leaves:** If an employee is on approved medical leave, military deployment, or statutory paid parental leave during the first quarter of the year, their March 31 expiration deadline is extended by an amount equal to the duration of their approved leave.

### 4.3 Maximum Accrual Balance Caps (1.5x Annual Tier Allowance)
To prevent the indefinite stockpiling of PTO balances, GlobalTech enforces a maximum accrual cap equal to **1.5 times the employee's annual tier allowance**:
- **Tier 1 Cap:** 180.0 hours (22.5 days).
- **Tier 2 Cap:** 240.0 hours (30.0 days).
- **Tier 3 Cap:** 300.0 hours (37.5 days).
Once an employee's total accumulated PTO reaches this cap, all further semi-monthly PTO accrual ceases immediately. Accrual will resume on the next payroll cycle after the employee takes paid time off and brings their balance below the maximum ceiling. Retroactive accrual credits for pay periods spent at the cap are not provided.

### 4.4 Cash Payout Restrictions & Separation Rules
- GlobalTech does not offer voluntary cash buyouts or "cash-ins" for accrued PTO during active employment.
- Upon voluntary resignation or involuntary separation of employment, accrued, unused PTO will be paid out on the final paycheck only in jurisdictions where mandatory payout is required by state statute (such as California, Colorado, Illinois, and Massachusetts). In non-mandated jurisdictions, unused balances are forfeited upon departure.

## 5. Sick and Medical Leave Guidelines

### 5.1 Frontloaded Annual Paid Sick Leave (10 Days / 80 Hours)
- GlobalTech provides all regular full-time employees with **ten (10) paid sick days (80 hours)** on January 1 of each calendar year.
- Sick leave is frontloaded separately from general vacation PTO and is immediately available for use on Day 1 of employment (prorated for mid-year new hires).
- Paid sick days may be utilized for:
  1. The diagnosis, care, or treatment of an existing health condition or preventative medical/dental care of the employee.
  2. The care of an immediate family member (child, spouse, domestic partner, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law) requiring medical diagnosis, treatment, or convalescence.
  3. Mental health wellness days, counseling sessions, or recuperation from acute stress or exhaustion.
  4. Safe leave related to seeking medical attention, legal assistance, or safety planning related to domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking.

### 5.2 Medical Documentation Requirements (>3 Consecutive Days)
- To preserve the integrity of our sick leave program, employees absent for more than **three (3) consecutive business days** due to illness or injury must provide a written note or medical certification from a licensed healthcare provider confirming their temporary inability to perform work duties and stating their anticipated return-to-work date.
- The medical note should not disclose specific confidential medical diagnoses; it only needs to verify professional medical evaluation and certify fitness-for-duty.

## 6. Comprehensive Paid Parental Leave Policy

GlobalTech is proud to offer a market-leading, gender-neutral, inclusive paid parental leave benefit to support new parents during one of life's most transformative milestones.

### 6.1 Eligibility & Benefit Allowances
- **Primary Caregiver Leave:** Eligible regular full-time employees who have completed at least six (6) continuous months of service and who assume primary responsibility for the day-to-day care of a newborn, newly adopted child, or newly placed foster child receive **sixteen (16) weeks (80 business days) of 100% fully paid parental leave**.
- **Secondary Caregiver Leave:** Eligible full-time employees assuming secondary caregiver responsibilities receive **eight (8) weeks (40 business days) of 100% fully paid parental leave**.
- In the event of dual-employee households where both parents are employed by GlobalTech, both parents are entitled to their full individual caregiver allocations.

### 6.2 Utilization Window & Structuring
- Parental leave must be commenced and completed within the **twelve (12) month window** immediately following the date of the qualifying birth, adoption, or foster placement.
- **Flexible Structuring:** Parental leave may be taken in one continuous 16-week or 8-week block, or divided into two discrete blocks of no fewer than two (2) weeks each, coordinated with the employee's manager and People Partner.
- While on paid parental leave, employee benefits (medical, dental, vision, life insurance, 401(k) eligibility) continue uninterrupted, and standard PTO accrual continues.

## 7. Bereavement and Compassionate Leave

GlobalTech extends heartfelt support to employees experiencing the loss of a loved one. Paid bereavement leave is granted based on family relationship tiers:

### 7.1 Immediate Family Bereavement (5 Consecutive Business Days)
- Employees are granted up to **five (5) consecutive paid business days** of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member: spouse, domestic partner, child, stepchild, parent, stepparent, legal guardian, sibling, or stepsibling.

### 7.2 Extended Family Bereavement (3 Consecutive Business Days)
- Employees are granted up to **three (3) consecutive paid business days** of bereavement leave for the death of an extended family member: grandparent, grandchild, mother-in-law, father-in-law, brother-in-law, sister-in-law, aunt, uncle, niece, or nephew.

### 7.3 Travel Accommodations & Additional Unpaid Leave
- If travel exceeding 500 miles one-way is required to attend funeral services, employees may request up to two (2) additional days of accrued PTO or unpaid compassionate leave with manager approval.

## 8. Departmental Operational Blackout Periods

To protect customer service level agreements (SLAs), financial compliance, and peak commercial revenue periods, GlobalTech enforces temporary departmental PTO blackout windows:

### 8.1 Finance, Accounting & Tax Blackout Windows
- **Quarterly Close:** The **last five (5) business days** of each fiscal quarter (March, June, September, December).
- **Annual Audit & 10-K Filing:** The **first ten (10) business days** of Quarter 1 (January).
- During these blackout windows, all non-emergency vacation PTO for finance, accounting, payroll, and internal audit staff is frozen.

### 8.2 Sales & Customer Success Year-End Blackout
- **Q4 Revenue Close:** The **final seven (7) business days** of Quarter 4 (late December).
- Sales account executives, solutions architects, sales engineers, and customer renewal managers must maintain availability to execute contract signatures and customer onboarding.

### 8.3 Engineering Operational Code Freezes
- Engineering enforces an annual production code freeze between December 20 and January 3; however, engineers are explicitly encouraged to take vacation during this time, leaving only designated on-call rotation engineers.

### 8.4 Blackout Exception Protocols
Exceptions to blackout periods for once-in-a-lifetime events (e.g., weddings, milestone graduations) require a formal written Exception Request submitted at least sixty (60) days in advance, signed by the respective **Department Vice President** and the **Chief People Officer**.

## 9. Civic Duty, Voting, and Military Leave

### 9.1 Jury Duty & Court Appearances
- Regular employees summoned for mandatory jury duty or subpoenaed as a non-party witness receive up to **ten (10) business days of full pay** per calendar year.
- Employees must provide a copy of the official jury summons to People Operations upon receipt.

### 9.2 Voting Leave
- Employees whose work schedules do not provide at least two (2) consecutive non-working hours while polling stations are open are granted up to **two (2) hours of paid time off** at the beginning or end of their scheduled shift to cast their ballot in official municipal, state, or federal elections.

### 9.3 Military Leave (USERRA)
- GlobalTech provides unpaid leaves of absence and job restoration rights to all employees who enter the uniformed military services of the United States, National Guard, or armed forces reserves in full compliance with the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA). GlobalTech provides salary differential pay for up to thirty (30) days of annual reserve active-duty training.
